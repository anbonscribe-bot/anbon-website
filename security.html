Security & Privacy at Anbon AI Scribe

Anbon AI Scribe is designed with the privacy and security needs of healthcare organizations in mind. Our approach is intended to help healthcare providers meet their obligations under Ontario's Personal Health Information Protection Act (PHIPA).

Protecting Patient Information

Patient information is protected using encryption during transmission and while stored. Access is restricted to authorized users and is controlled through secure user accounts.

Responsible Use of Data

Patient information is used only to provide and support the AI Scribe service. We do not sell patient information or use it for advertising.

Patient audio, transcripts and clinical notes are not used to train public AI models.
