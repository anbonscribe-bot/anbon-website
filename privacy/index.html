Website Privacy Notice

Anbon Systems Ltd. · Last updated: October 16, 2026

This notice explains how Anbon Systems Ltd. handles personal information in connection with its public website at anbonsystems.ca, including the software download page. It is not a complete privacy notice for the separate Anbon Scribe software service or for patient information processed through that service.

Information you provide

If you contact us by email or request a demonstration, we receive the contact details and messages you choose to provide. These may include your name, email address, organization, and the information you enter in a booking request.

Website operation

When you visit the website, our website-hosting and delivery services may process technical information such as IP addresses, browser details, requested pages, and access or security logs to deliver the site and help maintain its reliability and security.

Demo booking and external services

Our website embeds a Calendly booking widget for scheduling demonstrations. Information you provide through that widget is handled by Calendly under its applicable privacy practices and may be shared with us so we can schedule and respond to your request. Our website also links to software downloads hosted on Microsoft Azure storage. Those services may process technical information when you use them.

How information is used

Information associated with the public website is used to answer inquiries, arrange demonstrations, provide requested information, deliver software downloads, and operate and protect the website.

Service providers and disclosure

Our website relies on third-party hosting, download-delivery, and scheduling services. Information may be processed by these providers as part of delivering their services. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law.

Retention and security

Retention periods depend on the type of information, why it was provided, and operational or legal needs. We take steps intended to protect information, but no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your questions and requests

For questions about information you have provided through the public website, or to request access, correction, or deletion where applicable, contact eugene@anbonsystems.ca. We will review requests in accordance with applicable requirements.

Clinical software and patient information

Anbon Scribe is a separate healthcare software product. The handling of patient and clinical information through that product depends on the service configuration and applicable agreements and privacy requirements. Please contact us for the relevant product privacy and security documentation. Do not send patient health information through general website inquiries or demo booking forms.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our website or practices change. The date above indicates when this page was last updated.
